About

I grew up between Bolivia and the United States, and my work has followed that same path across borders. After earning my B.S. in International Business and Marketing from the University of Maryland's Robert H. Smith School of Business, I served two years as a Community Economic Development Volunteer with the Peace Corps in rural Costa Rica, where I partnered with local institutions and youth entrepreneurs on projects the community will carry forward long after my service.

Today I'm based in Washington, D.C., where I coordinate business development for FunSalud, a health-focused foundation, and volunteer with UN Volunteers in support of Bolivia's National Committee for the International Year of Volunteers 2026. I work in English, Spanish, and French, and I care most about work that builds local capacity rather than dependence.
